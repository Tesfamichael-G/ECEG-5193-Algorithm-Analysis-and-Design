## What you will learn

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-database: **Understand database concepts and architecture**

    ---

    File-based systems vs. DBMS, the ANSI-SPARC three-schema architecture, multi-user DBMS, and the database development life cycle.

- :material-table: **Master the relational model**

    ---

    Relations, tuples, attributes, domains, keys, entity and referential integrity constraints, schema vs. instance.

- :material-sitemap: **Design databases conceptually**

    ---

    E-R modelling: entities, attributes, relationships, cardinality, participation, weak entities, and EER constructs (specialisation, generalisation, categories).

- :material-arrow-decision: **Map conceptual models to relational schemas**

    ---

    The 7-step ER-to-relational mapping, the 9-step EER-to-relational mapping, and informal design guidelines.

- :material-shield-check: **Apply normalization**

    ---

    Functional dependencies, Armstrong's axioms, closure, minimal covers, and the normal forms 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF.

- :material-code-tags: **Query databases with SQL**

    ---

    DDL (CREATE, ALTER, DROP, constraints) and DML (SELECT with joins, aggregates, GROUP BY, subqueries, EXISTS, UPDATE, INSERT, DELETE).

- :material-harddisk: **Understand storage and indexing**

    ---

    Disk structure, file organisation (heap, sorted, hashed), dynamic and extendible hashing, and RAID levels.

</div>

## Chapters

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-numeric-1-circle: **Introduction**

    ---

    File-based systems and their limitations; the database approach; DBMS components; ANSI-SPARC architecture; multi-user DBMS; development life cycle.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 1](chapters/01-introduction.md)

- :material-numeric-2-circle: **The Relational Model**

    ---

    Data models; relations, tuples, attributes, domains; keys; entity and referential integrity; schema vs. instance.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 2](chapters/02-relational-model.md)

- :material-numeric-3-circle: **Conceptual Database Design — E-R Modelling**

    ---

    Entities, attributes, relationships; cardinality and participation; weak entities; EER concepts (subclasses, specialisation, generalisation, categories); attribute and relationship inheritance.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 3](chapters/03-er-modeling.md)

- :material-numeric-4-circle: **Logical Database Design**

    ---

    The 7-step ER-to-relational mapping; the 9-step EER-to-relational mapping; informal design guidelines; functional dependencies; normalisation (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 4](chapters/04-logical-design.md)

- :material-numeric-5-circle: **Relational Algebra and Calculus**

    ---

    Unary and binary operations; set-theoretic operations; joins; division; tuple and domain calculus; examples on the COMPANY database.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 5](chapters/05-relational-algebra.md)

- :material-numeric-6-circle: **Structured Query Language (SQL)**

    ---

    DDL (CREATE, ALTER, DROP, constraints); DML (SELECT with joins, aggregates, subqueries, set operations, EXISTS, NULLs); INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 6](chapters/06-sql.md)

- :material-numeric-7-circle: **Disk Storage, File Structures, and Hashing**

    ---

    Disk storage devices; files of records; unordered, ordered, and hashed files; dynamic and extendible hashing; RAID technology.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 7](chapters/07-storage-hashing.md)

</div>
